GURU NANAK COLLEGE [Autonomous]
Accredited at 'A++' Grade by NAAC

The Guru Nanak College, (GNC) in a lush green 25-acre campus, cradled between the Raj Bhavan and the campus of the IT.Madras, facing the Guru Nanak Salai, is located in Velachery, South Chennai - the gateway to the IT expressway of Tamil Nadu.Guru Nanak College was established in 1971 to commemorate the 500th birth anniversary of Shri Guru Nanak Devji - the founder of the Sikh religion, by the Guru Nanak Educational Society (GNES).(Late) Lt. Col. G.S. Gill was the founder President, (Late) Padmashree P. N. Dhawan was the General Secretary and (Late) Sardar Manohar Singh Sabherwal was the Treasurer.This yeoman service of a small community of Sikhs, who made Tamil Nadu their home, after they left Punjab following the
partition is historic.
The noble mission of the GNES is to provide quality education to all, irespective of socio-economic background, caste, creed or religion. This is in keeping with the motto of the College 'Pro Bono Publico' which is a translation of Sikh prayer 'Sarbat ka bhalla'meaning for the 'Benefit of All'
Guru Nanak College in Velachery, Chennai, offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across arts, science, commerce, and management.

The college operates across multiple streams with the following notable programs:
 
Undergraduate (UG) Courses 

• Commerce: B.Com (General, Corporate Secretaryship, Accounting & Finance, Bank Management, Computer Applications, Honours, Marketing Management). 
• Science: B.Sc (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Plant Biology, Advanced Zoology, Visual Communication, Computer Science, Biotechnology). 
• Arts & Humanities: B.A (Defence & Strategic Studies, Economics, Political Science & Administration). 
• Management & IT: BBA (Retail Management, General), BCA. [2, 5]  

Postgraduate (PG) Courses 

• Arts: M.A (Economics, Defence & Strategic Studies, Tamil, English). 
• Science: M.Sc (Computer Science with AI, Chemistry, Mathematics, Zoology). 
• Commerce & Management: M.Com, MBA. 
• Computer Applications: MCA. 
• Social Work: MSW (Master of Social Work).  

Doctoral Programmes (Ph.D) 
Available in disciplines including Economics, Defence & Strategic Studies, Chemistry, Zoology, Commerce, Tamil, and English.   
Skill Development Courses 
Through the Guru Nanak Centre for Skill Development, the college also offers short-term courses like AWS Cloud, Cyber Security, Kotlin App Development, and Advanced Tally.
